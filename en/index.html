Features

Serial scanning

Scan products back to back; quantities are grouped automatically. Wrong scan? One tap on "undo last scan".

Bulk shelf moves

Scan the target shelf, scan the products, one approval: all locations are updated. Move the scanned quantity or all stock, split across several shelves if needed.

Product lookup

Search by EAN, Stockitup product code or SKU and instantly see on which locations and in which quantities the product is registered.

An unfinished count is never lost: continue on the same or another device. A count lock keeps two devices from getting in each other's way.

Does Stockitup list other products on this shelf that you did not scan? Loclume shows them, even for a partially counted shelf.

Fast mode

No waiting for Stockitup while scanning: keep scanning, and one safety check runs for the whole count at the summary.

Multiple stores

Several stores or warehouses in one organisation, with permissions per location. Switch workspace with one tap.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does Loclume replace Stockitup?
No. Stockitup remains your main system for stock and e-commerce. Loclume is the operational layer on the floor: counting, scanning, comparing and resolving differences safely. Approved corrections are written back to Stockitup.
Which scanners can I use?
Three options: your phone camera, the same Loclume Android app on a scanner terminal with a built-in reader (like a Zebra TC22), or an existing HID/Bluetooth/USB scanner that sends barcodes as keyboard input, ending with Enter. Serial scanning works with all three.
What if the warehouse wifi drops?
Your count stays safely stored on the device. As soon as the connection is back, Loclume syncs the scans and you continue counting, on the same or another device. The Stockitup comparison and corrections wait until everything is synced, so nothing is applied twice or wrongly.
What does Loclume cost?
You first try Loclume free for 7 days. After that €30 per month per company (excl. VAT), cancel monthly. Includes unlimited counts, shelf moves, Stockitup connection and audit trail.
How safe is my Stockitup connection?
Your API credentials are stored encrypted server-side after verification and can no longer be read from the app. Only an owner or admin can renew the connection. Every change to Stockitup is recorded with old value, new value, user and date.
Who is Loclume for?
For shops, warehouses and operations teams that work with Stockitup and want to count, move and reconcile physical stock quickly: from bookshops to webshop warehouses.
