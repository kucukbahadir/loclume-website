B. The Loclume app

Data your organisation records in Loclume (counts, locations, stock changes) is processed on behalf of that organisation. For account management, security and support, WeIntensify B.V. is itself responsible.

What data

  • Account: email address, user ID, name, role and workspace.
  • Counts: shelf codes, scanned EAN/SKU values, quantities, timestamps and which user did what.
  • Stockitup connection: connection status, seller ID, warehouse ID and approved stock changes. Your Stockitup credentials are stored encrypted on the server side and are never readable from the app.
  • Feedback: reports, status history, team replies and any diagnostics you send along.
  • Device: a local device ID used to coordinate counts safely and to recover after a connection failure.

Why

  • Sign-in and enforcing team, store and role permissions.
  • Running counts, comparing them with Stockitup and sending approved changes.
  • Showing recent activity, feedback status and the audit trail.
  • Investigating support requests you submit.

Legal basis: performance of the agreement with your organisation and our legitimate interest in a secure service.

What we do not do

  • No advertising SDKs and no cross-app tracking.
  • Camera images used for barcode scanning are never stored or uploaded; only the decoded code is used.
  • No Stockitup secrets or server keys inside the app.
